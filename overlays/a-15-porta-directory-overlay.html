Entrance
Mai Cao,Jiahe Wan, Nicole Bastidas

该项目被构想为庞贝古城的新入口，沿着埃尔科拉诺门外的墓园路延伸，直抵昔日的萨尔诺运河——这条古道曾连通庞贝与奥普隆蒂斯。项目既回应了当代交通可达性问题，又重塑了这条历史轴线作为重要商业与基础设施走廊的功能，重新激活了西北方向长期中断的连接通道。作为建筑与景观的双重干预，该入口重新界定了古城边界，在公共与私密领域、建筑形态与林地景观之间寻求平衡，并延续庞贝城的模块化逻辑。通过空间过渡、材质延续与景观设计，它构筑出全新的抵达体验，将当代流动性与历史延续性紧密相连。
The project is conceived as a new entrance to Pompeii, located along the extension of the Tombs Road outside Porta Ercolano and reaching the former Canal di Sarno, an ancient route once connecting Pompeii to Oplontis. Responding to both present-day accessibility issues and the historical role of this axis as a major commercial and infrastructural corridor, the project reactivates a long-interrupted connection from the north-west. As an architectural and landscape intervention, the entrance redefines the boundary of the ancient city, negotiating public and private realms, built form and woodland, and the modular logic of Pompeii. Through spatial transitions, material continuity, and landscape design, it frames a renewed experience of arrival that links contemporary movement with historical continuity.
