Originating from the MACHINE HOUSE CONCEPT WORKSHOP, this project encourages students to explore the regenerative potential of recyclable materials using Large Language Models (LLMs). By leveraging AIGC technology, the project generates conceptual imagery that illustrates the practical applications of these newly developed materials. The entire experimental process and its outcomes are presented through an engaging creative concept titled "DABC Kitchen."
For more details, please visit: https://maiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiii.github.io/maicao.github.io/dabc-kitchen.html
